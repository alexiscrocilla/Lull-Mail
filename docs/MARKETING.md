# Lull: Marketing voice & manifesto

> Reference document for product communication.
> All public copy (website, README, Product Hunt, app stores, social
> posts) should align with the voice and angles defined here.

---

## One-line positioning

> **Lull. Your inbox, on mute.**
> Silent by default. Notification by exception. Your data stays yours.

---

## Voice rules

1. **Never lead with "AI", "agentic", "smart".** AI is the means,
   not the promise. It's mentioned in the feature list, never in
   the title or the subtitle.
2. **User benefit first.** Calm, silence, focus, control, privacy.
   That's what we're selling.
3. **No empty superlatives.** No "revolutionary", "next-gen",
   "game-changing". Short sentences, concrete facts.
4. **Radical honesty.** We say what the product is *not*. We
   acknowledge it doesn't suit everyone.
5. **Clean prose in any language.** Avoid lazy borrowings: in
   French, prefer "boîte mail" to "inbox", "notification" is fine,
   "pusher" is not. In English, avoid AI-jargon unless we're
   talking to engineers.

## Long pitch (homepage / product page)

### You get 80 emails a day. Three of them actually matter.

The problem isn't your inbox. It's what we've turned it into:
a permanent feed, a digital leash, a doorbell that rings for the
twentieth newsletter from a site where you bought a phone case in
2019.

Lull sits between your mail accounts and you. It reads everything
that comes in, picks out the three or four messages that genuinely
deserve your attention, summarises them in two lines, and sends
*one* notification: the right one, at the right moment.

The rest waits until you have time. Or quietly disappears.

### What Lull does

**Reads for you.** Every email is classified (important,
transactional, newsletter, promo, spam) and summarised in two lines.
Importance score from 1 to 10. You see at a glance which message
deserves three minutes and which deserves three seconds.

**Pushes without bothering you.** A notification only fires on your
phone *when* something genuinely urgent lands in one of your
mailboxes. For everything else, radio silence. Your phone gets to
be a phone again.

**Cleans up continuously.** Detects newsletters you haven't read in
months. Spots the unsubscribe link in one click. Suggests automatic
rules: move, mark read, delete. Nothing breaks.

**Lives on your machine.** No cloud. No €12/month SaaS. No account
to create with us. Lull is a native app you run on your machine that
talks straight to your IMAP servers.

### What Lull is NOT

- **Not a new email client.** Keep using Outlook, Gmail Web, Apple
  Mail or whatever you like. Lull works alongside.
- **Not a new address.** It plugs into your existing accounts:
  Gmail, Outlook/Microsoft 365, iCloud, Yahoo, ProtonMail
  (via Bridge), Orange, OVH, Free, or any IMAP server.
- **Not a cloud.** Your credentials never leave your machine. The
  database lives locally on your drive. Want to wipe everything? One button.
- **Not a product that watches you.** We don't sell your emails.
  We don't index them to target ads. We do nothing except what's
  written on the box.

### Who it's for

- Freelancers and self-employed people with four mailboxes and
  zero appetite to babysit a fifth.
- Professionals who spend their day in meetings and just want to
  know if something important landed.
- People who eventually muted all email notifications, and now
  miss things that matter.
- Anyone who wants their data on their own hard drive. Not in
  someone else's data centre.

### Two-minute setup

1. Download the installer for your platform (Windows / macOS / Linux).
2. Connect your mail accounts (guided wizard).
3. Paste the notification topic into the ntfy app on your phone.

That's it. Lull runs in the system tray. You forget about it.
That's exactly the point.

## Manifesto

### Email is fifty years old. We let it become a leash.

Every day, dozens of messages compete for your attention at the
same volume, whether it's a client waiting on a reply, a bank
confirming a transfer, or a sock company that decided Tuesday was
a great day for an inventory clearance. It's absurd. We've accepted
the absurd.

**Lull is built around four convictions.**

#### 1. Attention is rare. A notification has to earn it.

Silence is the default. A message that buzzes on your phone has to
have earned the right to do so. Everything else waits until you
choose to look.

#### 2. Your data belongs to no one but you.

Your credentials stay on your machine. Your emails stay on your
machine. No intermediary servers, no "Lull account", no telemetry.
Privacy by construction, not by promise.

#### 3. We don't migrate. We filter.

Your accounts already exist. Your contacts already have your
address. Nobody wants to change email. Lull plugs into what already
works and adds calm. A layer, not a replacement.

#### 4. Local software hasn't said its last word.

A program running on your hard drive is faster, more private, and
cheaper than a service running on ours. No subscription, no API
outage, no "your service ends December 31st". You install it, it's
yours.

**Lull won't make you more productive.**
**It will make you calmer. Which, these days, is infinitely more valuable.**
